Print safety
5 mm side margins are built in. Only 48 mm of your 58 mm paper is printable. Horizontal designs automatically rotate 90° when exported, so they still fit the roll. The back prints as a separate slip, not on the reverse of thermal paper.

Print your cards

We'll prepare pages at the exact 58 mm paper width. Choose Save as PDF in your browser's print window, or print to your SUNMI directly.

For accurate PDF sizes: use Chrome or Edge, set Scale to 100%, Margins to None, and turn off headers and footers. The PDF page size stays 58 mm × your chosen length, including when the artwork is horizontal.
